# Privacy Policy — Fahhemhom (فهّمهم!)

**Effective date: November 25, 2026**

**Application:** Fahhemhom (فهّمهم!)

**Developer:** Bilal B. S.

**Contact:** bilal1988@gmail.com

**Android package:** com.fahhemhom.game

## 1. Overview

Fahhemhom is an Arabic social party game designed for local multiplayer entertainment. The application provides offline gameplay, optional local-network television connectivity and optional one-time purchases of additional content packs.

We respect your privacy and aim to minimize the collection and use of personal information.

## 2. Account Registration

Fahhemhom does not require users to create an account or register with an email address, telephone number or password.

Google Play purchases are handled through Google Play using the user's existing Google account.

## 3. Locally Stored Information

The application may store information on your device to support its functionality, including:

- User-entered team names.
- Game settings and preferences.
- Scores and saved matches.
- Previously displayed card history.
- Drawing data associated with saved gameplay.
- Purchase verification information and cached content entitlements.

This information is used to provide gameplay, resume matches, manage content availability and maintain access to purchased content.

## 4. Local Network and Television Connectivity

Fahhemhom provides an optional feature that allows users to connect a compatible television or another device through a web browser on their local network.

When this feature is used, gameplay information, including team names, scores, timers, permitted game content and drawing strokes, may be transmitted to the connected display.

The television feature is designed to operate through a local network without requiring a dedicated developer-operated cloud server.

Local network communications may not be encrypted. We recommend using a trusted private Wi-Fi network or personal hotspot.

## 5. In-App Purchases

Fahhemhom offers optional one-time purchases of additional game content.

Purchases and payment processing are handled by Google Play Billing.

The application may access purchase records, transaction verification information and product ownership status to unlock purchased content, verify ownership and restore purchases.

The developer does not directly receive or process payment card numbers or banking credentials.

Google's handling of information is subject to its own privacy policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6. Advertising and Analytics

Fahhemhom does not contain advertising or use dedicated third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Google Play and its associated services may process technical, transaction-related or diagnostic information in accordance with Google's applicable policies.

## 7. Data Use and Sharing

Information handled by the application is used for purposes such as:

- Providing gameplay functionality.
- Supporting local television connectivity.
- Saving and restoring matches.
- Managing card history and preferences.
- Verifying purchases and restoring purchased content.
- Maintaining application reliability and security.

The developer does not sell users' personal information.

Gameplay information may be transmitted to a display selected by the user. Purchase-related information may be processed through Google Play services.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information may be retained on your device until it is cleared, replaced or deleted.

Users may reset card history through the application's available settings.

Users can also remove locally stored application data through Android's application settings or by uninstalling the application, subject to Android backup and restoration behavior.

Google Play purchase records are managed separately by Google. Deleting local application data does not cancel valid purchases.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Fahhemhom is intended primarily for users aged 13 and older.

The application does not require account registration or knowingly solicit personal information from children.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of the application and any in-app purchases.

## 10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect information handled by the application.

However, no method of electronic storage or network transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should connect only to trusted local networks and compatible displays.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the application, its functionality or applicable requirements.

Updated versions will be published on this page.

## 12. Contact

For questions concerning this Privacy Policy or the application's data-handling practices, please contact:

**bilal1988@gmail.com**
